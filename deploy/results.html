Cross-Simulator Results — Full Quaternion-Based Attitude Control for a Quadrotor

Fresk & Nikolakopoulos (ECC 2013) controller, gains frozen (Pₛ=20, Pω=4, ±4 N·m), flying the same paper vehicle on two independent physics engines · Group 12, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

Headline: with identical gains, references, and the paper's ±0.1 noise model, step and sine tracking agree across engines to within 0.2° RMS (17.7° MuJoCo vs 17.7° PyBullet) — the loop belongs to the controller, not the engine. The 360° flip completes deterministically on MuJoCo (airborne, 3 seeds) and hits an engine-dependent limit cycle on PyBullet — analyzed in the repo (P² pursuit equilibrium, Eq. 33).
