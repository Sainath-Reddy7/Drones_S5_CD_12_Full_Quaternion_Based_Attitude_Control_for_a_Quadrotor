## What was proposed (FRP.md)

Take the paper's quaternion attitude controller (Fresk & Nikolakopoulos, ECC
2013) — **gains frozen**: P&#8347;=20, P&#969;=4, ±4 N·m — and fly it on **four
simulation stacks**: gym-pybullet-drones, MuJoCo, Gazebo, ArduPilot SITL, with
one shared bridge, one telemetry schema, one cross-simulator benchmark.
Milestone M1 = bridge + the two native adapters; M2 = Gazebo + ArduPilot.

## Headline results

1. **Cross-engine agreement**: identical gains/references/noise give step &
   sine RMS within 0.2° between MuJoCo and PyBullet (17.7°/17.7°; 17.6°/17.5°)
   — the closed loop belongs to the controller, not the engine.
2. **The flip, honestly**: completes deterministically on MuJoCo (airborne);
   engine-dependent limit cycle on PyBullet — *derived* as the P² pursuit
   equilibrium ω = 5·sin(e/2) (Eq. 33), not hand-waved.
3. **Three derived contributions** (Eqs. 31–33 in `file_structure.md`): the
   q_paper = conj(q_sim) simulator convention (test-proven), PX4-style
   priority mixer desaturation (measured necessity), and the flip
   rate-equilibrium analysis.

Full benchmark table + 8 figures: repo README (results section), or
**quadrotor-quaternion-sim.vercel.app** — machine-generated by
`python -m sim.compare` / `sim.site_gen`, nothing hand-edited.
